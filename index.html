The challenge we solve

Your Product Managers work hard. Are they building the right things?

AI is handing your teams more speed every quarter, and speed multiplies whatever direction you give it. Your teams are working at full effort. What they are missing is a shared standard for how to envision the work, execute it, and prove it mattered. You have bought training before, and it produced two weeks of energy and no change in behavior. Closing that gap for good is what every engagement is built to do.

01

Tickets without a strategy

Your Product Managers are writing tickets. Upstream, nobody wrote the strategy, so four backgrounds aim four different ways and the work gets executed well and pointed at nothing.

02

It all lives in one person

One person on your team does this beautifully. Everything that person knows walks out the door with them, and the standard leaves at the same time.

03

Releases, where leadership wanted results

Ideas ship without an expected outcome, a measurement plan, or a read out afterward. Data driven gets said in every meeting and shows up in none of them.

04

A prioritization fight, every quarter

With no weighted scoring, the loudest stakeholder wins the quarter and strategy quietly leaves the room.

It arrives quietly, as a year of motion the business never felt.

Why our approach works

One shared mindset.

North · the platform

The engagement ends. The standard stays.

North is Product Protégé's platform. Five things change once it is in place. Each one below carries the mechanic that produces it and the screen it comes from, and nothing here is a mock.

01

Every piece of work is connected to the strategy above it, structurally.

North holds vision, strategy, pillar, metric, priority, roadmap, epic and story as one connected graph rather than a folder of documents. Day to day, a Product Manager cannot write an epic that floats free of a pillar and a metric.

What it kills

The quarterly scramble where a director spends three days rebuilding a deck to answer why are we funding this. The answer is already in the system, traceable in seconds.

Metric Tree. Measurable outcomes organized by strategic pillar, with King KPIs and the signals beneath them.
Prioritization Hub. Decision matrix and MoSCoW side by side, so priority is a position in the graph rather than an argument in a meeting.
02

Quality of thinking becomes a number, so it stops being one leader's opinion.

Every artifact carries a Readiness Score with a Readiness DNA breakdown across Problem Clarity, Customer Focus, Future State, Boldness, Values, Alignment and Urgency. The four tiers run Insufficient Signal, Developing Clarity, Stakeholder Ready, Saturation Point.

What it kills

The senior person rewriting their team's work at 10pm the night before the review. The Product Manager sees the gap and closes it before it ever reaches an exec. Review cycles collapse, and the work shows up stakeholder ready the first time.

User Stories. Epics broken into deliverable stories, each one scored as it is written rather than after someone complains about it.
03

Coaching happens inside the work, at the moment of the decision.

Protégé Coach runs structured seven-question wizards. Debate Me challenges the reasoning against the team's own vision and roadmap. Knowledge Gate quizzes sit in front of export, so people prove they understand what they are shipping before it leaves the building.

What it kills

The training problem every L&D and workforce leader already knows: the workshop scores well and nothing changes on Tuesday. The learning lands at the moment of the task instead, which is what makes capability persist after the engagement ends.

Influence Matrix. Power against interest, so the Empower phase is practised on the real stakeholder map rather than described in the abstract.
04

Leaders get a view of the portfolio they have never actually had.

The Manager Dashboard and the Global Roadmap roll individual work up into team alignment, overlaps, orphaned items and who is improving over time. It is the same graph the Product Manager works in, read from above.

What it kills

The status meeting where every team reports green. A VP sees two teams building the same thing before the money is committed, and sees who is thin on which dimension before that gap shows up in a failed launch.

The roadmap, two reads. Now, next, later for commitment, and the same roadmap read against time by quarter.
05

The intelligence is grounded in one methodology and one corpus, and it never trains on your data.

North runs on the E3 Product Mindset, refined across years of coaching and hundreds of coaching transcripts that feed the retrieval layer behind every wizard, every challenge and every score. That is the part a competitor cannot copy.

Security, plainly

Context injection only. No training on customer data. Multi-tenant isolation. Terms built into the product. That sentence closes more security conversations at this stage than a certificate does.

Metric Marketplace. 176 pre-built metric templates, AI-guided discovery, or a custom metric with its own formula.
Plenty of AI tools will write the document for you. North engineers the thinking behind it.

Why start here

Most product investments start with a hunch.

A leader senses the team is capable of more, buys training or tooling, and hopes the two overlap. Sometimes they do. The Diagnostic removes the hoping. You see the gap in your own team's answers, in your own team's artifacts, before a single dollar goes toward closing it.

What it includes

Five parts, in this order.

01

The scored exercise

Every Product Manager works through the full exercise, scored across all eight product themes, and receives a curiosity and execution archetype that explains how they approach the work.

02

The cohort view

We roll the individual reads into one picture of your team. Where the standard is strong, where it is thin, and which gaps are one person versus all of them.

03

Discovery conversations

One-hour conversations on real artifacts and real trade-offs, because a score tells you what someone knows and a conversation tells you what they do under pressure.

04

The findings readout

A one-hour session where we walk you through what we found, person by person and theme by theme, with the evidence behind every claim.

05

The engagement plan

A tailored plan for what to do about it, sequenced and sized to your budget. Take it and run it yourself if you want. Plenty of leaders do.

What you walk away with

An aggregate review of your team.

  • A baseline you can measure against twelve months from now
  • Per-person one-pagers you can use in development conversations
  • A named list of the two or three gaps actually costing you delivery
  • A plan sized to your budget, with nothing bundled you did not ask for
