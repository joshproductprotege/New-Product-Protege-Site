# Content & language rules (locked)

The Product Protégé brand voice follows strict rules. Keep to them when editing any copy.

## Never use
- The word **"assessment"** — use "snapshot", "diagnostic", or "exercise".
- The word **"free"** — use "included".
- The **"PM"** acronym — always spell out "Product Manager(s)".
- **Em-dashes** (—) or **semicolons** in body copy. Rewrite as separate sentences or commas.
- Emojis in body copy.
- Antithesis / corrective-reframing constructions ("It's not X, it's Y").
- Marketing fluff and hype. Prefer plain, direct, active voice and second person ("you").

## Always
- Active voice, direct "you".
- Spell out "Product Manager" / "Product Managers".
- Keep sentences human and specific.

## Sacred terms — reproduce exactly, never reword
- **E3 Product Mindset** (the three phases: Envision, Empower, Elevate)
- **Product Empowerment Pyramid**
- **Protégé Library** (accented "é")
- **Protege Pulse** (the newsletter — intentionally NOT accented)
- **Product Thinking Diagnostic** (the full, team-wide evaluation run with prospective clients)
- **Product Mindset Snapshot** (the quick, ~2-minute self-guided version)

## Snapshot vs Diagnostic — keep the distinction explicit
- **Snapshot**: self-guided, ~2 minutes, instant, no email required to start. The quick taste.
- **Diagnostic**: run by the Product Protégé team for prospective clients. Scores every
  Product Manager and the whole cohort, adds discovery conversations, produces a findings
  readout and a tailored engagement plan.

## Team framing
Product Protégé is **led by founder Jason Abdo and delivered by a team of seasoned product
leaders**. Avoid one-person-army framing. The other coaches have each launched many products
and share a passion for helping Product Managers hone their craft.
